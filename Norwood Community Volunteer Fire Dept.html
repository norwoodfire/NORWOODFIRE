Our Mission

"To protect life and property in our community from loss due to fire, medical or any emergency in which we may help, and serve the public with pride, integrity and compassion to the best of our ability."

NON-TAX SUPPORTED

Protecting the Norwood Community

The Norwood Community Volunteer Fire Department is the primary responder for over **160 square miles** of our community. However, we are an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit and do not receive property tax allocations or public budget funds. Our ability to serve this vast area depends entirely on community memberships and donations.
