Our Mission

"To protect life and property in our community from loss due to fire, medical or any emergency in which we can be of service."

NON-TAX SUPPORTED

Protecting the Norwood Community

The Norwood Community Volunteer Fire Department is the primary responder for over 160 square miles of our community. However, we are an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit and do not receive any city, county, or tax funding. We rely entirely on membership fees, donations, and fundraisers to operate.
